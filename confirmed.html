Before we talk

Three things that make the call worth your time.

Nothing to prepare, nothing to send. Just have the real numbers in your head.

  1. 01

    Your numbers.

    The liquid capital you could invest, and how you would fund it: cash, an SBA loan, a 401(k) or ROBS, home equity. Rough is fine. We will start there.

  2. 02

    Your timeline, and the role you picture.

    When you would want to open, whether you would keep your job at first, and whether you see yourself running it day to day or managing a team.

  3. 03

    Your questions.

    Brands you are already looking at, industries that pull you, and anything that worries you about ownership. Nothing is off the table, and the red flags are part of the conversation.
