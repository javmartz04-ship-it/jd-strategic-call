Is this call for you?

Business ownership is not for everyone, and that is exactly what the first call is for. Here is who it tends to work for.

This is for you if

  • You are a professional, executive, or investor seriously considering ownership, now or in the next year or two.
  • You have, or can access, around $50K or more in liquid capital. Most opportunities I work with start there.
  • You want a straight answer more than you want a sales pitch.
  • You are teachable, and willing to follow a proven system instead of reinventing it.

This is not for you if

  • You are looking for passive income with no involvement, especially early on.
  • You have already decided on a brand and want someone to tell you only the good parts.
  • You would rather trust a ranking than do your own diligence.
